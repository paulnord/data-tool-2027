# Boost.Math Landau approximation

`src/core/fit/landauCoefficients.ts` and the rational PDF evaluation in
`src/core/fit/landau.ts` adapt the 53-bit Landau PDF from
[Boost.Math landau.hpp](https://github.com/boostorg/math/blob/develop/include/boost/math/distributions/landau.hpp).
The source-file checksum and retrieval date are recorded beside the coefficients.
The TypeScript adaptation adds analytic derivatives and a mode/scale conversion.
